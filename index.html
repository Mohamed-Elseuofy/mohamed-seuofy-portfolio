Professional Bio

I am a results-driven mechanical engineer, energy efficiency leader, hydrogen researcher, and professional trainer with over 15 years of experience in the oil and petrochemical industries. My work focuses on transforming engineering knowledge into practical improvements that enhance equipment reliability, reduce energy consumption, minimize downtime, and support measurable operational and financial value.

As a Certified Energy Manager, Certified Energy Auditor, and ISO 50001 Lead Auditor, I support energy management systems, energy audits, greenhouse gas reporting, carbon footprint calculation, and decarbonization programs. My PhD and M.Sc. research connect industrial heat transfer, cryogenic hydrogen, green hydrogen systems, and advanced hydrogen liquefaction technologies.

I also deliver technical training and mentoring programs for engineers and industrial teams, helping organizations build internal capabilities in energy performance, rotating equipment, ISO implementation, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement.

Instructor Training Topics – Energy, Sustainability, and Decarbonization

As an instructor, my training portfolio focuses on energy transition, energy efficiency, renewable energy, decarbonization, and industrial energy systems. The training topics are designed to combine international standards, practical implementation, technical calculations, and real industrial applications.
